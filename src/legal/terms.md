## In short

- GarageAI is a marketplace. It connects people who need AI inference ("buyers") with
  independent people and companies who run open AI models on their own hardware ("operators").
- GarageAI does not own the hardware, does not run the models and does not write the answers.
  Operators are independent: not our employees, agents or subcontractors.
- AI answers can be wrong. You decide what to do with them.
- The service is in beta and provided as it is. We do our best, and we limit our liability as far
  as the law allows.
- Do not send sensitive or personal data through the marketplace until verified garages and
  data-processing agreements exist.

## 2. What GarageAI does, and what it does not

GarageAI provides a portal, an API gateway and the Bridge software. Through them, a buyer's
request is routed to an operator's machine, where an open model produces the answer, and the
answer is returned to the buyer. GarageAI measures usage, takes payment from buyers and, when
payouts exist, pays operators.

GarageAI does not:

- own, run or control operators' hardware or the models on it;
- create, review, store or take responsibility for prompts or answers;
- license the models. Each model is published by a third party under its own licence, which
  applies to its use.
